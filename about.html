About Baba Falooda

Bringing You Iconic Indian Desserts Since 1986

Our Story

Born in the heart of Mumbai, Baba Falooda has been serving smiles since 1986. From a single outlet to a beloved name with branches across Mumbai and even London, we’re famous for our flavorful faloodas, rich kulfis, and overloaded milkshakes.

Whether it's a family outing, late-night craving, or weekend treat – Baba Falooda brings you a taste of nostalgia, crafted with love and tradition.

Why You'll Love Us

  • 35+ years of legacy and quality
  • Delicious fusion of tradition & creativity
  • Instagram-worthy presentation
  • Massive menu – kulfis, rabri, falooda & more
  • Outlets in Mumbai & abroad
